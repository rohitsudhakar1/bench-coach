CAMERA SETUP

Calibrate both views.

Fix both cameras, focus and zoom. Use the printable checkerboard: 10 × 7 squares, 9 × 6 inner corners. Print at 100% (270 × 195 mm), mount flat, and measure the 25 mm squares.

  1. For the first pair, lay the board flat on a level surface. This sets gravity.
  2. Keep the entire board visible in both views. Hold still, capture, then vary tilt by 20–40°, depth and position. Keep corner orientation consistent.
  3. Collect at least 12 distinct poses; 20–25 is better. Compute, review the error, then use the good fit.

Corners are drawn on the exact sampled frames. Preview updates at most twice a second; it pauses while computing. Delivery timestamps do not prove synchronized exposure.
