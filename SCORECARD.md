## The weakest part

Seeing lab actions in real video. The default draw and dispense rule rarely fires (recall about 0.1). The --new-draw hints find most draws, but about half of them are false, and dispense hints are only a little better than chance. That is why hints never tick a step on their own. Touch and tip events on the held-out frames are close to useless, and vision never names a specific tube outside table mode, which has only been tried on drawn pictures.

## Not in the table

- Speech: the coach's noise gate and Whisper on the spoken test questions. The coach part's own tests cover them; this script does not load Whisper.
- The whole demo end to end with every part running, and how fast events reach the page live.
- Anything with a key: the Claude check on hints, the Claude note wording, the Claude protocol converter, Gemini Live, Nemotron. Every row here is the no-key behaviour.
- Real cameras, a real bench, real calibration, and any real user.

## How the numbers are made

Action events match a labelled action of the same kind within 1.5 s of its start and end, each action once; events during labelled mixing count neither way. The two 414-445 views run as one two-camera stream. Frame events count as right when a labelled frame within 0.8 s shows them. Chance moves the same events to random times in the clip (200 tries for vision, 50 for hint questions). These are small samples, not estimates of live accuracy.
